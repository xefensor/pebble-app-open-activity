# Open Activity privacy

Open Activity reads compatible Pebble activity summaries to resolve the app associated with the current activity. Its Launch Apps permission is required for the requested opening action. Ordinary app launches show setup and do not start an opening attempt.

Activity identifiers and launch tickets stay in transient memory and are checked against the current attempt and source validity. Open Activity stores only its verified display preferences on the watch and its configuration choices through Pebble's settings storage. It does not persist activity content, destinations from completed attempts or notification text.

Open Activity requests no location, music, notification or Internet permissions and has no account service, advertisements, analytics or developer-operated server. Activity providers and Pebble have their own privacy policies. Revoke permissions or remove the app through Pebble. Keep private source content out of support reports.
