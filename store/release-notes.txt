Open Activity 1.0.1

First stable preparation for Pebble Time 2 and Round 2.

Connect a supported Quick Launch shortcut to the Primary or Secondary activity currently displayed by Dynamic or Halftone. For example, return from a displayed countdown to Timer. The shortcut opens the associated app, not a specific saved item inside it.

Ordinary launches show setup guidance. With no current valid destination, the shortcut returns to the watchface. Pebble plugin support, a connected phone and Launch Apps permission are required.
