A button shortcut from the activity on your watchface to the app behind it.

See a Timer countdown on Dynamic or Halftone, then use Quick Launch to open Timer. Open Activity connects that shortcut to the activity currently displayed by your chosen watchface.

- Choose Dynamic or Halftone as the source watchface.
- Select its large Primary activity or smaller Secondary row.
- Assign different supported Quick Launch shortcuts to the two roles.
- Open the app from the watch's app list whenever you want its setup screen.
- Choose the display appearance and Small, Medium, Large or Follow Pebble text in phone settings.

Set up the same button shortcut in Open Activity and Pebble's Quick Launch settings. A shortcut opens the associated app, not a particular saved timer or item inside it. If there is no current activity with a valid installed destination, it returns to the watchface.

Requires Dynamic or Halftone, the official Pebble phone app's plugin support, a connected phone and Launch Apps permission. The source activity must provide an app to open. You can still open Timer directly from the app list without this helper.

Free. For Pebble Time 2 and Round 2.
