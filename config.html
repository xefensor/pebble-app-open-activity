Setup & help
  1. Install the current Dynamic build.
  2. Allow Open Activity to launch apps in the Pebble phone app.
  3. In Pebble’s Quick Launch settings, assign this same Open Activity app to each shortcut you want to use.

Use one connected watch. This opens Dynamic’s recent selection, including briefly after changing watchfaces.

A missing or expired activity returns to the watchface. It never substitutes Primary for a missing Secondary.

These choices apply when the shortcut is assigned to Open Activity. Other Quick Launch assignments stay as you set them.

Open Activity from the watch’s app list stays open for setup. Open its settings in the Pebble phone app, then press Back on the watch when finished. Assigned Quick Launch shortcuts still open activities automatically.

Back cancels while Opening… is shown. A launch already sent to the phone may still finish.
